Lasagna

Description

Lasagna is a classic Italian dish made with layers of pasta sheets, rich meat sauce, creamy béchamel or ricotta cheese, and melted mozzarella. The layers are baked together until the cheese turns golden and bubbly, creating a delicious combination of flavors and textures.

This comforting dish is perfect for family dinners or special occasions. Each bite brings together tender pasta, savory sauce, and gooey cheese, making lasagna a favorite for people who enjoy hearty, homemade meals.

Ingredients

  • 6 uncooked lasagna noodles
  • 3 skinless, boneless chicken breast halves
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese, divided
  • 1 (8 ounce) package cream cheese, softened
  • ¼ cup hot water
  • 1 cube chicken bouillon
  • 1 (26 ounce) jar spaghetti sauce
